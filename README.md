# Global Sales Performance Dashboard

[![Tool](https://shields.io)](https://microsoft.com)
[![Data Source](https://shields.io)](https://kaggle.com)

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track, analyze, and visualize global e-commerce sales performance. This dashboard transforms raw multi-regional retail data into actionable business intelligence by breaking down revenue streams across geographic regions, product categories, and buyer segments.

---

##  Key Insights & Metrics

* **Core KPIs**: High-level visibility into total sales revenue ($94K), generated profit ($7.57K), and total item units sold (2K quantities).
* **Geographic Distribution**: A detailed donut chart analyzing sales across over 10 distinct global regions, spearheaded by the Central and South territories.
* **Product Performance**: Vertical bar chart evaluating revenue performance across primary inventory categories (Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture).
* **Demographic Segmentation**: Proportional breakdown of the consumer base, classifying transactions into Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
* **Regional Breakdown**: Horizontal metric bars mapping high-volume sales drivers within specific states and provinces (e.g., Lower Saxony, Wellington, England).
